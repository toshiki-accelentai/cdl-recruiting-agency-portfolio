            How these metrics are calculated
            Calls received
            Call records saved in this desk during the selected business dates, including repeat calls. Dates use the record creation time in America/Vancouver. Calls that never reach this desk are not included.
            Intake success rate
            Calls linked to an applicant with no review flag, divided by all recorded calls in the period. Success means an applicant was saved with no review flag; it does not confirm a booking or a hire. This is not Vapi's conversation success evaluation.
            Callback requests
            Calls with a saved callback date and time, divided by all recorded calls for the percentage. A requested time does not prove a confirmed calendar booking.
            Zoho sync rate
            Calls marked sent, divided by calls with a tracked sync status: sent, pending, or failed. Calls without a sync record are excluded. Pending calls can still sync later.
            Readiness and follow-up
            Current profile status of unique applicants who called during this period. Repeat calls count once. These are current workload snapshots, not historical status changes or hiring outcomes.
            Additional voice metrics
            Call duration, response latency, cost per call, and provider-evaluated conversation success are not available in the current call feed. They are not estimated here.
